Announcing a post is its own small chore: the same story has to be retold six different ways, in six different lengths, in six different tabs. The **Share** tab does the retelling for you — and only the retelling. Agentimus never posts anywhere on your behalf.

## Where to find it

In the post editor, open the **Agentimus** panel (the box that also holds JSON-LD and Readability) and pick the **Share** tab. You'll find one card per network — **X (Twitter), Facebook, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Telegram and Reddit** — plus the post's link preview beside them, drawn from the same card image a real share would show.

## What each card holds

A ready-to-post draft written **from the post itself** — its title, its AI description, its topics — shaped to the network it's for: a short post for X, a title-plus-description for Reddit, a message that reads like a message for WhatsApp and Telegram. No AI is involved in these drafts; they're assembled locally, instantly, from words you already wrote.

Each card has two actions:

* **Copy** — the draft lands on your clipboard.
* **Open** — the network's own composer opens with the draft prefilled, where the network allows prefilling. Facebook and LinkedIn don't accept prefilled text — for those, **Open copies the draft first** and the card says so: paste when the composer appears.

## The AI polish (optional, per card)

With an AI provider set up (Settings → AI in WordPress 7.0+), every card gains a **polish** action: one call rewrites that one draft — tighter, in the network's register — and you can keep either version. No provider, no button; nothing here ever requires AI.

## Turning it off

Settings → Discovery → Features → **Share drafts**. The toggle removes the tab and closes its endpoint — off means off everywhere. Editor-only either way: readers never see any of this.
