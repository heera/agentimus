# User Manual

Everything you need to run Agentimus as a site owner — no code required. These pages walk you from installing the plugin to getting every feature working, in plain language.

Agentimus does two things: it makes your site **legible and citable to AI**, and it lets the **AI tools you already use operate your site** over an opt-in MCP server. Both sides are covered below.

New here? Read them roughly in order:

1. **Overview** — what Agentimus does and why it helps.
2. **Installation** — get set up in a couple of minutes.
3. **Dashboard & activity** — your AEO/GEO score, AI traffic, and what agents are doing.
4. **Report** — what AI did on your site between any two dates: today, yesterday, a week, a month, or dates you pick.
5. **Agent Access** — who authenticates to and *acts on* your machine surface (approved assistants, connection tokens, application passwords, abilities), and what was refused.
6. **Readiness** — the checks behind your score, plus the per-page Optimize worklist, with one-click fixes.
7. **Machine-readable outputs** — llms.txt, Markdown, discovery docs, sitemap.
8. **Structured data** — the Schema.org (JSON-LD) your pages emit, plus the one-line AI description behind each page's summary.
9. **Identity** — tell AI who you are and what you do.
10. **Topics for AI** — say what each page is about.
11. **Write with AI** — let your configured AI draft the descriptions, topics and fixes.
12. **Agent preview** — see exactly what an agent receives.
13. **MCP server** — connect Claude, Claude Code, Cursor, ChatGPT or Codex to your site by approving them: let them read your reports, and (opt-in) draft, edit and publish content and apply fixes.
14. **AI access & crawlers** — who may crawl, and how.
15. **Exposure & hardening** — optional privacy tightening.
16. **AI Visibility** — track whether AI engines cite you.
17. **Caching & CDNs** — keeping a page cache out of the way of the AI files.
18. **Privacy & data** — what leaves your server (almost nothing).
19. **FAQ** — quick answers to common questions.
